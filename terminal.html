How to read it

  • Gamma flip: above it, dealers are long gamma and trade against the move (chop, pinning). Below it they're short gamma and trade with the move (trends, air pockets).
  • Call wall: the strike with the most call gamma. It usually acts as a ceiling until it breaks.
  • Put wall: the strike with the most put gamma. It usually acts as a floor.
  • Peak GEX: the biggest single gamma strike. Price gets pulled toward it, especially into expiry.
  • Max pain: the strike where the next expiry pays option holders the least.

Method

Every Deribit BTC/ETH option with open interest. Gamma from Black–Scholes using Deribit's mark IV and each expiry's forward price (r = 0, expiry 08:00 UTC). GEX = Γ × OI × S² × 1%, calls positive and puts negative: the standard assumption that customers buy options and dealers sell them. Refreshes every 60 s, computed in your browser. What this is not

  • Not actual dealer books. The sign convention is an assumption, and some customers sell options.
  • Deribit only. It's the largest crypto options venue, but CME, OKX, Bybit and on-chain options are excluded.
  • Open interest updates as trades happen. Levels move intraday.
  • News tone is a count of bullish vs bearish words in the headline, not a judgment. Headlines come from public RSS (CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, Decrypt, The Block, Federal Reserve, Google News), refreshed hourly.
  • Educational, not investment advice.
